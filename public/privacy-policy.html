Cadence Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Cadence is a music player for your own self-hosted Jellyfin server. It is built to keep your data yours: it has no backend of its own, shows no ads, and does not track you or sell your information to anyone.

The short version

  • Cadence connects only to the Jellyfin server you tell it to. Your music, playlists, and listening data live on your server, under your control.
  • The only things Cadence stores on your device are your Jellyfin session (so you stay signed in) and your app preferences (such as theme and playback settings).
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Information you provide

To use Cadence you enter your Jellyfin server address and your Jellyfin username and password on the sign-in screen. These are sent directly to your Jellyfin server to authenticate you. Cadence stores the resulting session token on your device so you don't have to sign in every time; it is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

How your music and data are handled

Your library, playlists, liked songs, play history, and audio all stream directly between your device and your Jellyfin server. Cadence is a front end only — it does not route your music or listening activity through any server operated by us, and it keeps no copy of your library. Music you choose to download for offline listening is stored locally on your device and can be removed at any time from within the app.

Data stored on your device

Cadence keeps the following locally on your device (and nowhere else):

  • Your Jellyfin session token and the server address you entered.
  • App preferences: theme, playback speed, sort choices, recent searches, and similar.
  • Any songs, albums, or playlists you explicitly download for offline listening.

Clearing the app's data, or signing out, removes this information from your device.

Optional diagnostics

Cadence includes an opt-in diagnostics feature (off by default) that can record playback events on your device to help troubleshoot playback problems. It does nothing unless you enable it in Settings → Diagnostics. Uploading those diagnostic logs for support is a separate, explicit action you take — nothing is sent automatically. The logs contain technical playback information (such as track ids, timing, and error codes), not personal content, and are used solely to diagnose issues.

Third parties

Cadence does not include advertising or third-party analytics. When you request missing music or search, those requests go to services you have configured on your own server (for example, Jellyfin's search or a music-request backend you run). Cadence itself does not share your data with any third party.

Children's privacy

Cadence is a general-audience utility for accessing your own media server and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
